# 📊 E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis Using Tableau

### An Interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard for Sales Performance, Customer Behaviour, and Product Analysis

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis Dashboard** is a data visualization project developed using Tableau to explore sales performance and related business metrics.

The dashboard brings together key performance indicators (KPIs) and multiple visualizations to help users examine sales across product categories, months, geographic locations, customers, payment methods, genders, and age groups.

The main objective is to present business data in a visual and understandable format that can support data exploration, performance monitoring, and informed decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Monitor total sales, total profit, total orders, and total quantity.
- Compare sales across different product categories.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Explore geographic sales distribution across Indian states.
- Identify products with comparatively high sales.
- Compare sales by gender.
- Analyze customer-level sales performance.
- Examine sales associated with different payment methods.
- Understand sales distribution across age groups.
- Demonstrate the use of Tableau for business intelligence and data visualization.

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following visualizations.

### 1. Sales by Category

A horizontal stacked bar chart compares sales across product categories, including Furniture, Electronics, and Clothing.

**Purpose:** To compare category-level sales performance.

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart displays sales for January, February, and March.

**Purpose:** To examine monthly sales patterns and changes over time.

### 3. Sales by State

A geographic map visualizes sales distribution across locations in India.

**Purpose:** To explore regional sales patterns and potential market opportunities.

### 4. Top 10 Products

A horizontal bar chart ranks products according to sales.

Visible product labels include Laptop, Sofa, Tablet, Smartphone, Study Table, Office Chair, Jeans, Smartwatch, and Shoes.

**Purpose:** To compare product sales and identify products that may require further performance analysis.

### 5. Sales by Gender

A horizontal bar chart compares sales associated with Female and Male customer groups.

**Purpose:** To explore demographic sales patterns.

### 6. Customer Sales

A horizontal bar chart compares sales associated with individual customers.

**Purpose:** To examine customer-level sales distribution.

### 7. Sales by Payment Method

A bar chart compares sales associated with UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, and Cash on Delivery.

**Purpose:** To understand the distribution of sales across payment methods.

### 8. Sales by Age Group

A bar chart compares sales across age groups.

**Purpose:** To examine how sales are distributed across the displayed age ranges.

### 9. Category-Wise Sales Distribution

A pie chart compares the sales contributions of Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture.

**Purpose:** To provide a visual comparison of category contributions.
